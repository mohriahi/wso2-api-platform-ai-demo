## Current account packages

|---|---|---|
| Essential | €0 | Debit card, mobile app, free SEPA transfers |

## Cards

- **Debit card** (Mastercard): included with every package. Contactless up to
  €100 a payment. Apple Pay and Google Pay supported.
- **Classic credit card**: 19.9% APR, limit up to €5,000, no annual fee.
- **Gold credit card**: 16.9% APR, limit up to €15,000, €60 a year, 1% cashback.
- **Replacement card**: free, arrives in 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery
  (next working day) costs €15.
- Freeze or unfreeze a card at any time in the app.

## Savings

- **Easy Saver**: 2.0% AER, withdraw any time.
- **Fixed Saver 1 year**: 3.0% AER, no withdrawals before the end of the term.
- **Regular Saver**: 4.0% AER on up to €300 a month, for 12 months.

## Loans and overdrafts

- **Personal loan**: 6.9% APR representative, €2,000 to €30,000, 1 to 7 years.
- **Arranged overdraft**: 11.9% EAR on the amount used.
- **Unarranged overdraft**: not allowed; payments that would take you over your
  limit are declined, with no fee.

## Payments and transfers

- **SEPA transfer**: free, arrives the next working day.
- **Instant SEPA transfer**: €0.50 on Essential, free on every other package,
  arrives in seconds, up to €15,000.
- **International (SWIFT) transfer**: €15, plus the receiving bank's fees.
- Your IBAN and BIC are shown in the app, under Account details.

## Using your card abroad

| Where | Card payments | Cash withdrawals |
|---|---|---|
| Euro countries | free | free |
| Everywhere else, Essential and Everyday | 2.75% of the amount | 2.75%, minimum €3 |
| Everywhere else, Plus and above | free | free, then 2% after €500 a month |

Exchange rate: the Mastercard rate of the day.

## Switching to {brand}

The account switching service moves your direct debits, standing orders and
salary from your old bank in 10 working days, and closes the old account if
you ask it to.

## Security

{brand} never asks for your full card number, PIN, or one-time passcode by
phone, email, text or chat. Report a lost or stolen card in the app at once:
